8 exercises. Here you find 7 of them: 

- Given a text with a study: Name the independent and dependent variables. 
Also specify whether the study is active, controlled or meta. 

- PCA: What happens to the eigenvalues and eigenvectors if one dimension is very large and what happens when in one dimension all elements have the same value. 

- There were 3 plots given with log x axes and / or log y axes. We had to find the function. For every function you could read of 3 x and y values. 

- We were given a default Gaussian probability distribution with mean 0. First we had to calculate the Maximum Likelihood Estimator for the variance, 
then we had to give the formal definition of the Fisher Information I_n and calculate the Fisher Information. Then we had to check, whether the estimator is asymptotically normal. 

- Entropy: Show that H(P(X,y)) = H(X) + H(Y)

- A plot with the probabilities of a binomial distribution and the survival-function was given. We had to read of the p-value. 
  If we had 400 cases with the same p-value, how many errors would we expect? 

- What are high level steps in data engineering, for each explain 1 problem.
